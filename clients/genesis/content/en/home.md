In 1990, Menese International Schools began its goal to establish an educational system focused on equity, quality, and distinction. Over time, the school has established a reputation for itself. Its long history places it among the reputable and well-established educational institutions in the country.

Now operating as Genesis International Schools, the school continues to prioritize equity and offers three different academic systems:

- **The Semi-International System** From Baby Class to IGCSE
- **The American System** From Pre-K to Grade 12
- **The British System** From FS1 to Grade 10

Genesis has inherited the legacy, history, and accumulated knowledge of Menese, carrying over 30 years of experience and expectations.

At Genesis, there is a wide range of activities and events that cater to students’ different interests and preferences. From sports to drama, public speaking, and music, there is always something for everyone. The school also organizes fun activities such as movie nights, field trips, and game tournaments to keep students engaged and entertained. In addition to this, edutainment events like science fairs, model United Nations, and STEM competitions help students enhance their knowledge and skills while having fun. With such a diverse range of activities and events, Genesis ensures that students have a holistic learning experience that is both enjoyable and enriching.
