Thanks to God Almighty then to the efforts of the Government of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the Ministry of Education, and all the concerted efforts. We will be able to overcome the exceptional circumstances that our students lived in due to the Corona virus pandemic Successfully.

Leaders’ Foundation International School has taken several ways of preparations for the new academic year, which included three main axes:

**Hygienic and precaution preparations:**

- Including hygiene procedures for all school building According to the guidelines of the [Ministry of Health](https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/Pages/Default.aspx).
- Installing temperature measuring devices.
- Provide medical masks for free.
- Maintaining the social distancing regulations inside classrooms, through keeping a distance of (1.5 to 2 meters) between students, as well as providing alcohol and hand sanitizers everywhere inside schools, and creating isolation rooms for any suspected case.

**Academic and educational preparations; include:**

- Providing virtual classrooms.
- Launching the virtual tours.
- – Collaborating with **Alpha Publishing** that is specialized in producing high-quality programs and resources for US K-12 education and English Language Teaching and Learning.
- provide books, e-books, and a digital LMS platform.
- Connect students and teachers, offering them a collaborative, interactive, mobile learning environment in the classroom or via distance learning at home.

**Facilities for parents:**

- The ability to register and upload documents and files online.
- 20% discount on the tuition fees
- Online payment.
- Providing interest-free installments system for the school fees.

**Leaders’ Foundation International School** **started receiving registration requests online, for all classes.**

**We wish all students in Leaders’ Foundation International School a happy and safe academic year and a fruitful educational journey.**
