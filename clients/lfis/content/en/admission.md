## Student Admission Procedures

<div class="gallery">

![](../../media/Img03.jpeg)

![](../../media/Img02.jpeg)

Admission to **Leaders’ Foundation International School** (LFIS) is open to students who can benefit from the educational services currently provided by LFIS’s programs. The school’s admission policy is to provide an outstanding English language, international educational program.

**Admission to applicants is open only to grades where space is available**. 
 The school will maintain its international character with English as the language of inclusion in order to achieve our mission and vision.

Admission is initially considered upon receipt of an online request submitting personal and financial reference information. LFIS reserves the right to invite a candidate to subsequently formally apply to LFIS.

Once formally invited to make an admissions application, acceptance to LFIS will depend on satisfactory submission of all documents/student records from previous schools and successful completion of LFIS’s screening and testing process.

The admission process becomes completed when the applicant accepts, in accepting, LFIS’s operating rules and regulations as well as meeting and completing **all financial requirements** and notices of acceptance by the Admissions Office.

**LFIS reserves the right for final admissions placement decisions.**

**Age requirements:**

The basic criteria for placement are the age of the child by September 2021, as decreed by the Ministry of Education and the school’s assessment of the child during the admissions process.
